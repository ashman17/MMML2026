## Terms used in the figures

- **Image motion** is the difference in normalized horizontal image position. **Scene motion** projects endpoint motion onto the first camera's right axis after bringing both points into that coordinate frame.
- **Still** means at most the specified horizontal threshold in absolute value. A target can rotate or move off axis and still qualify. The fresh small, moderate, and large bands use verified scene-motion magnitudes of (0.05, 0.20], (0.20, 0.50], and >0.50 simulator units.
- **Pair accuracy** means both reference-frame questions are correct on the same pair. **Balanced accuracy** in Ref-DAVIS17 is mean recall over the three direction labels. **IoU** is intersection divided by union for two masks.
- **Checkpoints and units** differ across protocols. Raw image/scene accuracy, Ref-DAVIS17 image-motion QA, and benchmark multiple-choice accuracy use different datasets and denominators.
